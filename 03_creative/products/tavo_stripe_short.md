# Creative Memory: Tavo Stripe Short

This document tracks the creative intelligence for the **Tavo Stripe Short**. It isolates what is working for this specific product to avoid mixing context with the Fluid Pant.

## 1. Performance Context (Aug 2026)

The Tavo Stripe Short is a breakout winner with **$9,534 spend** and a **2.10x ROAS** on the top-spending original ad ("1").

### Winning Creative Patterns
- **Text-Overlay on Photo:** The current CTR leaders (2.7–2.8% CTR) are on-model street photography with bold sans-serif headlines and explicit CTA buttons.
- **Proven Headlines:** "SUMMER BEST SELLER," "TEXTURED COMFORT," "TOP PICK FOR SUMMER."
- **Colorway Performance:** Cream is the clear winner (3.05x ROAS, 2.76% CTR).

### Underperforming Patterns
- **Flat-lays:** Product-only flat-lays with no text or direct ask are laggards (0–2.0% CTR). They read as editorial content rather than a "buy this" moment.

---

## 2. Creative Framework (Evolve Method)

### The "Micro-Demonstration" Shift
Apply the same cinematography-first prompting used for the Fluid Pant, but tailored to the Tavo's specific hooks: **Fabric Texture (Stripes)** and **Versatility (Beach to Street).**

### Prompting Strategy
- **Cinematographer Focus:** Direct camera behavior (waist-height tracking, low-angle architecture).
- **Fabric Hero:** Emphasize the vertical ribbed texture and how it catches light.
- **Call to Action:** Every video asset should be designed to support a bold text overlay and CTA button in post-production.

## 3. Active Briefs (FITZ-CR-0079 to 0088)

These concepts are organized by tier to test distinct angles for the Tavo Stripe Short.

### Tier 1: Movement Demo (Cold Audience)
*Testing the "fabric in motion" hook.*
- **FITZ-CR-0079 (Walk Cycle):** Waist-level tracking of cream shorts in urban street.
- **FITZ-CR-0080 (Stair Climb):** POV looking down at legs climbing concrete steps in black shorts.
- **FITZ-CR-0081 (Seated to Standing):** Knee-height transition from bench to movement in white shorts.

### Tier 2: Texture Close-Up (Product Discovery)
*Isolating the stripe texture USP.*
- **FITZ-CR-0082 (Fabric Touch):** Extreme close-up of fingers running across cream ribbed texture.
- **FITZ-CR-0083 (Light Play):** Macro shot of black fabric with moving golden hour light.

### Tier 3: Lifestyle & Showcase
- **FITZ-CR-0084 (Morning to Afternoon):** Quick cuts across kitchen, market, and cafe.
- **FITZ-CR-0085 (Beach to Street):** Transition from barefoot on sand to sneakers on sidewalk in white shorts.
- **FITZ-CR-0086 (Styled Flatlay):** Top-down reveal with sunglasses and watch props.
- **FITZ-CR-0087 (Rotating Product):** 180-degree rotation on white surface.
- **FITZ-CR-0088 (Editorial Lean):** Slow push-in on model leaning against concrete wall.
