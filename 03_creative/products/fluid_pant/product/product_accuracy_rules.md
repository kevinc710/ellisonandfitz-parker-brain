# Fluid Pant Product Accuracy Rules

## Purpose

This file prevents inaccurate product claims, visual mutation, stale commercial details, and creator misinformation. It is mandatory for all public-facing copy, briefs, statics, video prompts, and creator outreach.

## Accuracy status

| Information type | Status | Source to use | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fabric, fit, waist, care, and product description | **VALIDATED** | Live product page and Master Context | Use only approved product facts. |
| Current price, sale, colorways, sizes, availability, promotion, shipping, and return details | **DYNAMIC** | Live product page immediately before use | Never rely on a stored number or old screenshot. |
| Visual product fidelity | **VALIDATED** | Official product imagery and Visual Signatures | Supply current reference images to AI where accuracy matters. |
| Customer outcome and preference claims | **HYPOTHESIS** unless separately evidenced | Test result, review, or approved evidence | Do not state as fact. |

## Approved product facts

The following are supported by the current product page, subject to normal product-page updates.[1]

- Lightweight and breathable striped fabric.
- Relaxed straight-leg cut.
- Adjustable drawstring waist.
- Machine washable and quick drying.
- Suitable live-page use contexts include everyday wear, travel, and a casual night out.

## Public-copy rules

### Allowed when accurate

- Describe visible pleats, texture, drape, color shift, silhouette, waistband, and pockets.
- Use stable product facts listed above.
- Use a creator's honest first-person opinion if it is authentic and does not make a universal claim.

### Require a live check

- Price, sale, discount, code, or promotional offer.
- Current color, size, stock, or shipping statement.
- Product-page URL destination.
- Any new feature, bundle, or campaign statement.

### Prohibited without evidence and approval

- Best, most comfortable, perfect, guaranteed, or universal claims.
- Medical, athletic-performance, durability, or temperature-regulation claims.
- Comparison claims against another product or brand.
- Customer-result claims such as "everyone asks about these" or "makes you look more stylish."
- A claim that an asset is paid, sponsored, or approved for media use when it is not.

## Visual-accuracy rules

The Fluid Pant must retain its relaxed straight-leg silhouette, visible pleated micro-stripe texture, natural drape, and drawstring waist when visible. Do not add cargo pockets, belt loops, zippers, logos, cuffs, denim, athletic side stripes, leather, or unapproved colors.

Use official current product imagery for the selected colorway when generating or editing AI visuals. If a requested visual cannot preserve the product accurately, do not generate it as a final product asset. Label it concept-only until corrected.

## Creator rules

A creator may say what they genuinely notice, but must not invent technical specifications, delivery promises, discount amounts, or universal outcomes. Every creator post must use the current assigned link or code, correct disclosure, and only the rights approved in writing.

## QA before release

- [ ] Is every factual claim present on the current live product page or separately approved?
- [ ] Were price, offer, sizes, colors, shipping, and inventory live-checked if mentioned?
- [ ] Does the visual preserve the actual Fluid Pant silhouette and key physical features?
- [ ] Is the creator's content rights status clear?
- [ ] Does any subjective claim read as a first-person opinion rather than a customer fact?
