# Fluid Pant Colorways

## Dynamic-data rule

Colorway availability is **DYNAMIC**. The live product page is the only source of truth for what is currently offered, in stock, and shown with product imagery. Never use this file as a promise that a colorway is available.

**Live product page:** https://ellisonandfitz.com/products/ellison-fluid-pant

## Historical internal context

The Brand Brain records historical order-volume context for Mixed Color, Violet Blue, Royal Blue, and Fluorescent Green. This information is internal and time-sensitive. It must not be used in public creative, creator messages, or customer-facing claims.[1]

| Use case | Rule |
|---|---|
| AI generation | Provide the current official product image for the selected colorway. |
| Creator brief | Assign only a colorway confirmed to be currently available and approved for the campaign. |
| Static or video | Do not alter product color in post-production or generation without an approved reference. |
| Product copy | Do not state a colorway name, stock status, or size availability without a live check. |
| Paid testing | Log the specific colorway in the test because color can be a material creative variable. |

## Visual fidelity

A current colorway reference is mandatory when product color is central to the shot. Directional light may reveal tonal variation, but it must not turn the product into a different color or invent a gradient that does not exist in the official reference.
