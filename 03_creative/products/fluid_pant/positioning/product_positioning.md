# Fluid Pant Product Positioning

## Positioning statement

> **VALIDATED product expression:** The Ellison Fluid Pant is a lightweight, breathable, relaxed straight-leg pant with pleated micro-stripe texture, natural drape, and an adjustable drawstring waist. Its difference is visible in motion, light, texture, and silhouette.[1]

> **HYPOTHESIS positioning layer:** The Fluid Pant is for people who want ease without looking ordinary, and who value clothing that feels expressive, considered, and personally distinctive.

The first statement is product fact. The second is a strategic message to test, not an established customer truth.

## Category reframing

| Generic category frame | Fluid Pant frame | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A comfortable pant | A visually distinctive pant that is easy to wear | **HYPOTHESIS** |
| A fashion pant | A movement-led piece with visible texture, drape, and proportion | **VALIDATED** visual emphasis |
| A travel pant | A pant that can move through everyday wear, travel, and a casual night out | **VALIDATED** for live-page use contexts |
| A trend item | A product with a clear personal-style point of view | **HYPOTHESIS** |

## Message hierarchy

1. **First message:** The product looks different.
2. **Second message:** Here is the visual proof, such as pleats, drape, color shift, or silhouette.
3. **Third message:** Here is why that difference may matter in a real personal-style, movement, travel, or creative context.
4. **Fourth message:** Only when current and approved, present offer, code, or call to action.

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not lead with price, generic comfort, or a demographic label.
- Do not call the product luxury, premium, viral, best-selling, or universally flattering unless approved and supported.
- Do not make the creator's personality the product strategy. The visual product proof must remain central.
- Do not use another product's customer profile to define Fluid Pant positioning.
