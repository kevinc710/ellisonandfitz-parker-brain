# Fluid Pant Messaging Guardrails

## Core message rules

| Message element | Required rule |
|---|---|
| Product | Lead with a visible differentiator: pleats, drape, texture, color shift, silhouette, waistband, or pocket interaction. |
| Customer desire | Label as `VALIDATED`, `STRONG SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `UNTESTED` internally. Do not present a hypothesis as research. |
| Creator voice | Use natural first-person language and only opinions the creator genuinely holds. |
| Dynamic offer | Check live product page and current campaign brief before mentioning price, discount, color, size, shipping, or availability. |
| Audience | Personalize by visible context and content fit, not inferred demographic identity. |
| Rights | Organic affiliate content, paid UGC, and paid-media use are separate agreements. |

## Approved language directions

| Direction | Status | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product visual proof | **VALIDATED** | `Look at how the pleats move when you walk.` |
| Texture and light | **VALIDATED** | `The fabric catches the light differently from a basic pant.` |
| Personal style | **HYPOTHESIS** | `I do not want to dress like everybody else.` |
| Creative-life context | **HYPOTHESIS** | `I want what I wear to have something to say too.` |
| Everyday movement | **HYPOTHESIS** | `This is the kind of piece I would wear through a long day.` |

## Language to avoid

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| `The most comfortable pants you will ever own` | Unsupported universal claim |
| `Everyone asks me where I got these` | Unvalidated social-attention claim unless it is a real creator experience |
| `Perfect for every body type` | Unsupported universal-fit claim |
| `The best pants for travel` | Comparative claim without substantiation |
| `These will make you look more stylish` | Unvalidated customer outcome |
| Any fixed price, promotion, size, or shipping statement | Dynamic fact requiring current verification |

## Creator-message distinction

A creator can say: `These are some of the most interesting pants in my closet right now.` This is a personal observation.

A creator should not say: `These are the most comfortable or best pants you can buy.` This is a universal product claim.

## Internal-message distinction

Internal messages may discuss a `HYPOTHESIS` or `UNTESTED` audience angle. Public messages must not use that label or state it as established customer truth.

## No cross-product rule

Never borrow a winning Tavo Shorts message, customer desire, or offer angle and state it as a Fluid Pant messaging truth. If useful, record it as a Fluid Pant hypothesis with a product-specific test.
