## Core product idea

> **The Fluid Pant is not simply a comfort pant. It is an expressive, movement-led piece whose pleats, drape, texture, and silhouette are visible at a glance.**

The strongest creative work makes the viewer notice what makes the pant visually different before asking for a purchase.

## Claim guardrails

### Use freely when visually or factually true

- Lightweight and breathable.
- Relaxed straight-leg silhouette.
- Adjustable drawstring waist.
- Machine washable and quick drying.
- Pleated texture, drape, movement, and color shift.
- Styled for everyday wear, travel, or a casual night out.

### Use only as first-person opinion or real demonstrated experience

- "These are the most interesting pants in my closet right now."
- "I like how the fabric moves when I walk."
- "This is a piece I can see myself wearing when I travel."
- "I do not want to dress like everybody else."

### Do not use without explicit approval and evidence

- "The most comfortable pants you will ever own."
- Medical, athletic-performance, temperature-control, or durability claims.
- Claims about being luxury, premium, viral, best-selling, or a specific customer outcome.
- Exact price, discount, shipping time, inventory, or size availability unless live-checked.
- Claims that the product will fit every body, occasion, or style.
- Any comparative claim about another brand or product.

## Visual essentials

Every Fluid Pant asset should make at least one of the following visible:

1. **Pleats or texture:** A close, tactile view that makes the surface feel distinct.
2. **Drape or movement:** A stride, turn, wind, step, or fabric interaction.
3. **Silhouette:** A clear full look or low angle that makes the straight-leg shape readable.
4. **Color shift:** Light interacting with the fabric so the tonal detail reads.
5. **Pocket or waistband interaction:** A natural human gesture that grounds the product.
