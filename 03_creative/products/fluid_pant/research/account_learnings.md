## Required data imports

When adding a new learning, include:

- The Fluid Pant product tag or confirmed asset mapping.
- Platform, date range, and comparison set.
- Primary and secondary metric definitions.
- Test variable changed.
- Whether the conclusion is `VALIDATED`, `STRONG SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `UNTESTED`.
- The operating decision that changes because of the result.

## Contamination check

Before adding a learning, ask: "Would this conclusion still be true if we removed all data from other Ellison & Fitz products?" If the answer is no, record it as a Fluid Pant hypothesis with the external source named, or do not include it.
