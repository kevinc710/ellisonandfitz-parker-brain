## Data discipline

- Use only product-tagged Fluid Pant data.
- State the date range, platform, metric definition, and comparison set.
- Do not infer results from an ad name alone. Use the product tag or confirmed asset mapping.
- Do not import Tavo Shorts, Denver Knit, or other product results as Fluid Pant history.
- If a result is incomplete, record it as a `STRONG SIGNAL` or `HYPOTHESIS`, not `VALIDATED`.
