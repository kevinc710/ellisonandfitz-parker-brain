# Fluid Pant Visual Signatures

## Purpose

This file protects the visual identity of the Fluid Pant in statics, video, Nano Banana, Higgsfield, creator briefs, and editor work. It does not replace the live product page for current colorways or product images.

## The product signatures

| Visual signature | Status | What it must look like | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pleated texture | **VALIDATED** | Clearly visible vertical pleats that catch light or react to touch | Primary visual differentiator |
| Drape in motion | **VALIDATED** | Fabric moves naturally during a stride, turn, wind, or step | Makes the garment feel different from a basic trouser |
| Relaxed straight-leg silhouette | **VALIDATED** | Readable waist-to-hem or full-body proportion, not a skinny, tapered, or cargo-pant shape | Preserves product recognition |
| Drawstring waist | **VALIDATED** | Visible only when the shot naturally includes the waist; do not invent belt hardware | Product truth on live page |
| Striped fabric texture | **VALIDATED** | Fine all-over stripe or micro-stripe pattern, readable in detail or light | Product truth on live page |
| Color shift under light | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Directional light reveals tonal depth or color variation in applicable colorways | Documented priority hook and visual cue |
| Pocket interaction | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Hand enters or rests naturally in the pocket while the pant remains visually clear | Native, tactile product proof |

## Product fidelity rules for AI

### Preserve

- Relaxed straight-leg proportion.
- Pleated, textured, striped material.
- Natural drape and movement.
- Drawstring waist when the waist is visible.
- Product-dominant framing.
- Realistic fabric behavior under walking, turning, touching, and light.

### Do not invent

- Cargo pockets, zipper details, belt loops, logos, patches, side stripes, heavy hardware, or visible branding not shown in official product imagery.
- Skinny taper, jogger cuffs, tailored suit creases, denim texture, leather texture, parachute nylon, or athletic track-pant features.
- An exaggerated gendered or costume-like look that makes the pant less recognizable.
- Impossible color gradients, printed graphics, or new colorways unless a current approved reference image is supplied.
- Accessories that block the waist, pleats, silhouette, or movement.

## Framing rules

| Asset type | Preferred framing | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Texture static or video | Macro or waist-to-hem, directional light on pleats | Fabric too distant to read |
| Movement asset | Low angle, mid-stride, chest-down, or side profile | Upper-body or face-led framing where pant is secondary |
| Silhouette static | Full body or waist-to-hem against simple contrast | Busy backgrounds or dark clothing that hides the shape |
| Pocket or waistband asset | Close interaction with product dominant in frame | Hand or accessory covering product detail |
| Creator lifestyle asset | Full look plus clear product cutaway | Creator monologue without a legible pant shot |

## Reference-image rule

For AI generation, always provide the current official product image for the selected colorway when visual fidelity matters. The live product page controls the current available colors and product appearance.

## Required prompt language

Include the relevant phrase below in every image or video prompt:

```text
Preserve the Ellison Fluid Pant's relaxed straight-leg silhouette, visible pleated micro-stripe texture, natural drape, and adjustable drawstring waist. Do not add cargo pockets, belt loops, zippers, logos, cuffs, denim texture, or extra hardware. The pant must be the primary visual subject.
```

## Pre-generation check

- [ ] Current colorway reference is available if color accuracy matters.
- [ ] Pant proportion is relaxed and straight-leg.
- [ ] Pleats, stripe texture, drape, or silhouette will be readable.
- [ ] The selected setting does not hide the product.
- [ ] The assignment uses one visual signature, not an overloaded list of features.
