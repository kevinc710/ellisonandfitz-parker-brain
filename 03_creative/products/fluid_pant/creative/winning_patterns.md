# Fluid Pant Winning Patterns

> **Evidence discipline:** This file records product-specific creative patterns. A "winning" pattern may be `VALIDATED` only when the existing Fluid Pant record documents it as approved or proven. A hook prediction remains a `STRONG SIGNAL` or `HYPOTHESIS` until test metrics are logged.

## Current winning and priority patterns

| Pattern | Status | Evidence | Operating instruction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product-first micro-demonstration | **VALIDATED** | Existing Fluid Pant Creative Memory identifies micro-demonstrations as the core creative philosophy and lists product-led Tier 1 concepts. | Start with product proof, not generic setup. |
| Visible product differentiator | **VALIDATED** | Tier 1 and active concepts center on pleats, texture, silhouette, drape, and movement. | Build every asset around one visible differentiator. |
| Movement demonstration | **VALIDATED** | Movement Demo is named as a proven Tier 1 concept; movement frameworks are documented throughout the product record. | Use stride, turn, step, wind, or walking where it makes the drape readable. |
| Texture close-up | **VALIDATED** | Texture Close-Up is named as a proven Tier 1 concept and the source library prioritizes pleat detail. | Use macro or close texture when light makes the pleats readable. |
| Clear silhouette | **VALIDATED** | Multi-Angle Static product showcase is a Tier 1 concept; full silhouette is a central editor rule. | Use a readable waist-to-hem or full-body product frame. |
| Visual novelty or curiosity | **STRONG SIGNAL** | The hook tournament prioritizes extreme macro, color shift, low-angle stride, and delayed recognition; some rankings remain pre-test. | Test novelty-led hooks first, then log thumb-stop and 3-second hold. |
| Pocket and waistband interactions | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Documented as natural, tactile priority hooks with strong product proof. | Use as support or primary hook where the product reads clearly. |
| Premium but native execution | **VALIDATED** | Existing editor rules state that native feel and intentional imperfection should support, not undermine, product clarity. | Favor quick, real-feeling product discovery over slow commercial setup. |

## Pattern formula

> **Hook → one product proof → one reinforcing proof → clean end frame.**

The hook creates curiosity. The proof reveals the visual reason the Fluid Pant is different. The end frame closes on a readable silhouette, movement moment, or decisive product detail.

## Verified visual questions

Before calling an asset a winner, it must answer at least one of these questions clearly:

1. How do the pleats move?
2. How does the fabric catch light?
3. What makes the silhouette different?
4. What happens when someone walks?
5. What does the texture feel like?
6. Why would someone look twice?

## Metrics for promotion

| Pattern type | First metric | Second metric | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook test | Thumb Stop Rate | 3-second Hold Rate | Advance strongest hook into matched body edits. |
| Full ad test | Hold Rate or qualified view signal | CTR, CPP, or conversion metric appropriate to the campaign | Keep or revise based on product-specific comparison. |
| Creator organic asset | Product clarity and quality review | Click, code use, conversion, or audience response | Decide affiliate-only, revision, or paid-UGC consideration. |

A pattern should not be marked `VALIDATED` from aesthetic preference, a single strong comment, or a result from another product.
