# Fluid Pant Static Frameworks

## Rule

A static must make the Fluid Pant readable before a viewer reads the copy. Each static carries one product idea. Text, if used, reinforces the image rather than explaining a weak visual.

## Evidence note

A framework can be approved for production without being performance-proven. Do not treat `APPROVED FRAMEWORK` as equivalent to a proven winner. Promote a static route to `PERFORMANCE-PROVEN` only after Fluid Pant-specific ad results support it.

## Frameworks

| Framework | Evidence status | Visual setup | Optional copy direction | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silhouette First | **STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Full body or waist-to-hem frame with a relaxed, wide straight-leg proportion that matches the supplied product reference | `NOT YOUR USUAL PANTS` | Product becomes too small in a busy scene |
| Texture First | **STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Macro pleat or hand interaction under directional light | `LOOK CLOSER` | Texture is too distant or flat to read |
| Movement Freeze-Frame | **STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Mid-stride, turn, stair step, or wind moment where drape is frozen at its most readable point | `MADE TO BE NOTICED IN MOTION` | Still frame does not communicate movement clearly |
| Color-Shift First | **STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Close product crop under directional light showing authentic tonal variation | `LIGHT CHANGES EVERYTHING` | Color is exaggerated or no longer matches approved reference |
| Visual Puzzle | **STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Abstract macro crop where pleats or tonal variation create brief delayed recognition while the product remains relevant | `LOOK AGAIN` | Image becomes abstract without communicating pants |
| Architectural Product Hero | **HYPOTHESIS / APPROVED TEST** | Product-dominant waist-down or full-body frame in concrete, brutalist, gallery, corridor, or other minimal architecture | Usually no copy | Environment becomes the hero instead of the pant |
| Social Discovery / Message Screenshot | **HYPOTHESIS / APPROVED TEST** | Native-looking message or social format where the product image is the payoff | Use only claim-safe conversational copy | Simulated social proof is mistaken for real customer evidence |
| Styling Formula | **HYPOTHESIS** | Same pant styled across two or three real contexts | `ONE PIECE. YOUR WAY.` | Pant becomes too small, making the test about outfits rather than product |

## Static benchmark

Before approving a static, ask:

> **Does this image immediately communicate that these are not ordinary pants while making me curious enough to keep looking?**

The visual should create both **product relevance** and **curiosity**. A strange image that hides the product is not a useful Fluid Pant hook.

## Static build checklist

1. Pick one framework.
2. Pick one visual signature.
3. Select the official/current colorway reference when accuracy matters.
4. Decide whether copy is necessary. If yes, use one short line.
5. Review product dominance, claim accuracy, and readability at mobile size.
6. Confirm the pant shape still matches the reference: relaxed, wide straight-leg; never slim or tapered.
7. Name asset `FLUID_STATIC_[FRAMEWORK]_[ANGLE]_v01`.

## Copy rule

The image should still work with no copy. Do not use a headline to hide an unclear product or invent a desire. Avoid price, offer, shipping, universal comfort, or unsupported social-attention copy unless it is current and approved.

## Test design

When testing static variation, keep product colorway, offer, audience, and placement stable where possible. Change one visible variable, such as crop, hook, headline, or context. Log the test under [Testing History](../research/testing_history.md).
