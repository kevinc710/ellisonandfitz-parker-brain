# Fluid Pant Video Frameworks

## Rule

Every video opens with the pant or one visible product differentiator. The first second should create curiosity or prove a unique product property. Use one central product idea per video.

## Current frameworks

| Framework | Status | Sequence | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Discovery | **VALIDATED** | Macro texture → reveal waistband or silhouette → walking → wide end frame | Curiosity and progressive reveal |
| Movement | **VALIDATED** | Low angle → wide stride → turn → walk away | Drape, silhouette, and energetic motion |
| Texture | **VALIDATED** | Hand or pocket → pleats → waistband → walking | Tactile product proof |
| Architecture | **VALIDATED** as an operating framework | Minimal environment → side profile → walking → detail | Editorial context with product still central |
| POV | **VALIDATED** as an operating framework | Looking down → pocket interaction → walking → optional mirror | Native, wearer-perspective feel |
| Creator Identity | **HYPOTHESIS** | Product hook → 2 to 3 second natural creator statement → product proof | Testing individual expression or creative-life context |

## Timing guide

| Moment | Target |
|---|---|
| Hook | 0.0 to 1.0 seconds |
| First product proof | 0.5 to 2.0 seconds |
| Optional creator line | 1.0 to 3.0 seconds only after product appears |
| Reinforcing detail or movement | 3.0 seconds onward |
| End frame | Clean silhouette, walk-away, or decisive detail |

## Hook principles

- Start motion already in progress when using stride or walking.
- Use macro texture only if light and crop make the pleats intriguing.
- Keep product visible at 60% or more of frame when practical.
- Use a creator's voice only as a supporting layer, not a delayed product introduction.
- Hold the body edit constant when testing hook variants.

## Completed-testing versus priority distinction

The Product Discovery, Movement, Texture, Architecture, and POV structures are documented product-specific operating frameworks. The relative ranking of individual hooks within them is not conclusively established where the existing record marks ranks as predicted or pre-test. Treat individual hook priorities as `STRONG SIGNAL` until measured Fluid Pant results are logged.
