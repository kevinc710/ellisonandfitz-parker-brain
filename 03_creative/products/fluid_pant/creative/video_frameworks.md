# Fluid Pant Video Frameworks

## Rule

Every video opens with the pant or one visible product differentiator. The first second should create product-relevant curiosity. Use one central product idea per video.

## Evidence semantics

These are **product-specific operating frameworks**, not automatically performance-proven winners. A framework can be approved because it reliably demonstrates the Fluid Pant while its relative paid-media performance remains unknown.

Use:

- **PRODUCT-PROVEN / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** when the structure reliably demonstrates real product attributes.
- **STRONG SIGNAL** for a priority supported by Fluid Pant-specific analysis but not conclusive matched performance.
- **PERFORMANCE-PROVEN** only after completed comparable Fluid Pant tests establish performance.
- **HYPOTHESIS** when the structure is plausible but still requires a defined test.

## Current frameworks

| Framework | Evidence status | Sequence | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Discovery | **PRODUCT-PROVEN / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Macro texture → reveal waistband or silhouette → walking → wide end frame | Curiosity and progressive reveal |
| Movement | **PRODUCT-PROVEN / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Low angle → controlled stride → turn → walk away | Drape and silhouette |
| Texture | **PRODUCT-PROVEN / APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Hand or pocket → pleats → waistband → walking | Tactile product proof |
| Architecture | **APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Product-first low-angle or silhouette → minimal architecture → walking → detail | Editorial context with product still central |
| POV | **APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Looking down → pocket interaction → walking → optional mirror | Native wearer-perspective feel |
| Creator Identity | **HYPOTHESIS** | Product hook → 2 to 3 second natural creator statement → product proof | Testing individuality or creative-life context |

## Timing guide

| Moment | Target |
|---|---|
| Hook | 0.0 to 1.0 seconds |
| First product proof | 0.0 to 2.0 seconds |
| Optional creator line | 1.0 to 3.0 seconds only after product appears |
| Reinforcing detail or movement | 3.0 seconds onward |
| End frame | Clean silhouette, walk-away, or decisive detail |

## First-second benchmark

> **Which hook immediately communicates that these are not ordinary pants, while making me curious enough to keep watching?**

A visually interesting opening that could apply to any ordinary pant is not sufficient.

## Hook principles

- Start motion already in progress when using stride or walking.
- Use macro texture only if light and crop make the pleats intriguing while preserving believable fabric.
- Keep product visible at 60% or more of frame when practical.
- Prefer tight waist-to-shoe, waist-to-hem, low-angle, macro, and chest-down product framing.
- Use creator voice only as a supporting layer, not a delayed product introduction.
- Hold body edit, audio, offer, audience, and placement constant when testing hook variants wherever possible.
- For AI video, prefer restrained natural movement over dramatic fabric animation.

## Architecture correction

Architecture is a **secondary context**, not the hook by default. Do not open on an empty environmental establishing shot merely to signal premium. The pant or a Fluid Pant differentiator should remain the first visual read.

## Completed-testing versus priority distinction

The Product Discovery, Movement, Texture, Architecture, and POV structures are documented product-specific operating frameworks. Their existence does not prove that one framework or hook outperforms another. Predicted/pre-test rankings remain `STRONG SIGNAL` or `HYPOTHESIS` until measured Fluid Pant results are logged.
