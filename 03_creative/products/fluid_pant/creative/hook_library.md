# Fluid Pant Hook Library

## Rule

A hook is not a complete creative strategy. It is the first moment that earns the right to show the product proof. Each hook must communicate either curiosity or a visible Fluid Pant differentiator in the first 0.5 to 1.5 seconds.

## Hook candidates

| Hook | Status | Product proof | Use case | What is not yet known |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Extreme Pleat Macro | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Pleated micro-stripe texture and delayed recognition | Product Discovery | Whether it is the highest-performing hook versus matched alternatives |
| Color Shift | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Directional light reveals tonal variation and pleats | Product Discovery, Texture | Which light treatment and crop are strongest |
| Low-Angle Stride | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Wide-leg silhouette and drape under movement | Movement | Whether it beats other hook types at equal spend |
| Pocket Interaction | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Natural tactile gesture and product detail | Texture, POV | Relative performance versus stride or macro control |
| Waistband Reveal | **HYPOTHESIS** | Drawstring and pleat construction | Texture, product detail | Whether it works as a first frame or only in the body edit |
| Mirror | **HYPOTHESIS** | Full look plus wearer perspective | POV, styling | Whether mirror competes with product focus |
| Impossible Crop | **HYPOTHESIS** | Delayed recognition and visual puzzle | Product Discovery | Whether abstraction creates curiosity without losing product relevance |
| Walking | **VALIDATED** as product proof | Drape, step, and silhouette | Support shot and movement body edit | Whether it is stronger than other hooks as opening frame |

## Hook testing protocol

1. Use one body edit and one offer condition.
2. Change only the first 0.5 to 1.5 seconds.
3. Keep product, audience, placement, and end frame comparable.
4. Track Thumb Stop Rate first, then 3-second Hold Rate.
5. Only after hook performance is understood, assess CTR, CPP, and conversion outcomes.
6. Log results in [Testing History](../research/testing_history.md).

## Initial visual direction

The hook should answer one question instantly:

- What is that texture?
- Why does the fabric move like that?
- Why does the silhouette look different?
- What changes when the light hits it?
- Why would I look twice at this pant?

If the first frame could describe any ordinary pant, it is not a Fluid Pant hook.

## Do not do

- Open on a creator's face before product evidence.
- Use a generic lifestyle establishing shot.
- Use an unsupported product claim as a hook.
- Change hook, copy, offer, body edit, and audience at once.
- Use a non-Fluid Pant hook result as a Fluid Pant ranking.
