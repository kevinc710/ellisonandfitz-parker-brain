# Fluid Pant Learning Log

## Purpose

This log records conclusions that should change Fluid Pant creative, creator, or audience decisions. It prevents the team from repeating a discussion, losing a useful ChatGPT analysis, or treating a one-off opinion as a permanent rule.

## Entry standard

A useful learning contains a date, source, observation, decision, confidence, and next action. A raw transcript, unverified demographic claim, or vague preference does not qualify as a canonical learning.

## How to add a ChatGPT or research dump

1. Paste the material to the owner or agent with the date and source context.
2. Extract only the claims that are specific, testable, and useful.
3. Add the final conclusion here with a confidence level.
4. Update the relevant canonical file if the learning changes product truth, customer or creator fit, outreach, or creative operating rules.
5. Preserve raw source material outside this canonical directory if it is useful for audit or later review.

## Confidence definitions

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Supported by live product facts, repeated performance evidence, or an approved operating decision. |
| Medium | Plausible and strategically useful, but requires a defined test. |
| Low | Early idea or directional signal. Do not turn it into mandatory copy or a target persona. |
