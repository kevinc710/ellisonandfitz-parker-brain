# Fluid Pant Creator Outreach and Qualification

## Objective

Find creators who can make a credible, product-first Fluid Pant story for a relevant audience. Begin with an affiliate-only pilot. Move to paid UGC only after content quality, reliability, product fit, and paid-media rights are confirmed.

## Workflow

| Stage | VA action | Required output | Do not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Review public content, audience context, and recent short-form work | Candidate notes and relevant link | Promise payment, product, or UGC work |
| Personalized outreach | Use one real observation from the candidate's visible creative context | Initial message | Mention assumed demographics or make generic mass-pitch claims |
| Screening | Request handles, examples, audience summary, and availability | Screening response | Ask for sensitive details or shipping information before approval |
| Internal review | Score product fit, delivery, audience fit, and reliability | Advance, hold, or decline decision | Invite every interested candidate automatically |
| Affiliate pilot | Send UpPromote invitation, unique link, code, and one product brief | Activated portal and assigned brief | Promise paid usage or performance bonus |
| First-post review | Verify product accuracy, disclosure, link or code use, and creator quality | Approved post and next decision | Use the creator's asset in paid media without rights |
| UGC escalation | If selected, issue a separate paid UGC brief and rights agreement | Asset ID and written paid-media rights | Treat organic approval as paid usage approval |

## Universal initial outreach template

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME],

I came across your [SPECIFIC CREATIVE CONTEXT, SUCH AS STYLE, MUSIC, TRAVEL, OR CREATIVE WORK] and thought your point of view could make for an interesting Ellison Fluid Pant story.

The Fluid Pant is built around movement, texture, and an expressive silhouette. We are currently speaking with a small group of creators who can show the product naturally in their own world.

If you are open to it, could you send over:

1. Your active social links;
2. Two or three recent short-form examples;
3. A quick overview of your audience and primary platform; and
4. Confirmation that you would be interested in an affiliate pilot using a unique link and customer code.

If there is a fit, we will share the current product brief and next steps.

Best,
Ellison & Fitz Creator Team
```

## Personalization prompts for the VA

Use **one sentence only** before the standard message.

| Visible creator context | Personalization sentence |
|---|---|
| Musician or performer | `Your performance and creative background could make for an interesting Fluid Pant story around movement and personal style.` |
| Travel or lifestyle creator | `Your travel and everyday-style content could be a natural setting for showing how the Fluid Pant moves through a real day.` |
| Designer, artist, or stylist | `Your eye for materials and proportion could make for a strong Fluid Pant story around texture, drape, and silhouette.` |
| Founder or creative professional | `Your personal-style perspective could make for a strong Fluid Pant story about looking deliberate without dressing rigidly.` |
| Fashion or styling creator | `Your styling content could make for a useful Fluid Pant story around proportion, movement, and one piece worn multiple ways.` |

## Short audition request

Use this when the creator appears promising but the team needs to validate natural delivery before sending product or an UpPromote invitation.

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thank you for sharing your work. Before we make a product decision, we would like to see a quick, informal phone-camera audition. No editing is necessary.

Please record a 15 to 30 second vertical video and speak naturally, as if you are talking to a friend: What do you normally look for when choosing clothes, and what makes a pair of pants feel worth wearing to you?

There is no need to mention Ellison & Fitz or a specific product. We are simply looking for your natural delivery, point of view, and on-camera style.

Best,
Ellison & Fitz Creator Team
```

## Affiliate-pilot invitation template

```text
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thank you for sending your examples. We like your natural delivery and point of view and would like to invite you into our initial Ellison & Fitz affiliate pilot.

The first opportunity is an organic creator brief for the Ellison Fluid Pant. You will receive a unique affiliate link and customer code, with 10% commission on eligible referred sales. The brief focuses on a natural product story in your own voice.

This initial affiliate brief does not include paid-media usage or a performance bonus. Any separate paid UGC opportunity is discussed only after we have reviewed the first organic asset and agreed on written terms.

If you would like to proceed, please confirm and we will send your affiliate invitation and next steps.

Best,
Ellison & Fitz Creator Team
```

## Screening scorecard

| Criterion | Pass standard | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Product fit | Creator has a believable setting for movement, personal style, travel, or everyday expression | |
| Short-form quality | Can make a concise, clear vertical asset with usable sound and visual framing | |
| Natural delivery | Specific, conversational, and not overly scripted | |
| Audience context | Has a clear primary platform and audience explanation | |
| Reliability | Responds clearly and completes the requested next step | |
| Affiliate readiness | Willing to use the unique code and link with proper disclosure | |

## Information boundaries

- Do not request home address, payment details, tax information, or other sensitive data during screening.
- Do not share creator-specific commission exceptions, UGC caps, or paid-media bonus terms until a creator is selected and terms are approved.
- Do not frame outreach around a candidate's assumed age, income, relationship status, or identity.
- Do not say that paid UGC work is guaranteed.
