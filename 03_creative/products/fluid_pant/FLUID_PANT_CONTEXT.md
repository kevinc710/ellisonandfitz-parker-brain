# Fluid Pant Master Context

> **Read this first before doing any Fluid Pant work.** Every downstream customer, creative, research, prompt, creator, or operating file must be consistent with this document.

## Purpose

The Ellison Fluid Pant is a product-specific creative and growth system. This file turns product facts, performance analysis, creator discussions, footage analysis, and approved operating decisions into a controlled source of truth.

The objective is not to make every Fluid Pant asset look alike. The objective is to make every asset, creator brief, static, video, and AI prompt **congruent with the product's real visual differentiators and current evidence level**.

## Two-axis evidence system

Do not use one word to mean both "we approved this workflow" and "this won in paid media." Record **evidence strength** separately from **decision type**.

### Evidence strength

| Status | Meaning | How the team may use it |
|---|---|---|
| **VALIDATED / PERFORMANCE-PROVEN** | Supported by stable product truth or repeated/comparable Fluid Pant-specific performance evidence. | Use as product truth or a proven performance conclusion, depending on source. |
| **STRONG SIGNAL** | Supported by meaningful but incomplete Fluid Pant evidence or product-specific analysis. | Prioritize testing. Do not call it a conclusive winner or customer truth. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | Plausible, testable idea without enough Fluid Pant-specific evidence. | Use only in a defined test. |
| **UNTESTED** | Reasonable question with no product-specific evidence yet. | Do not use as a targeting fact or default claim. |

### Decision type

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PRODUCT TRUTH** | Verified physical/functional product fact. |
| **APPROVED FRAMEWORK** | Team-approved way to produce or structure work. It is not automatically performance-proven. |
| **OPERATING DECISION** | Approved process/rule for the team. It is not automatically a customer insight. |
| **PERFORMANCE RESULT** | Conclusion based on measured Fluid Pant ad/creator performance. |

A file may therefore say `STRONG SIGNAL / APPROVED FRAMEWORK`. Never simplify that to "validated winner."

## Absolute rule: no cross-product data contamination

> **Tavo Shorts, Denver Knit, other Ellison & Fitz products, competitor observations, and general apparel ideas may inspire a Fluid Pant hypothesis. They may never be recorded as Fluid Pant customer or creative truth until Fluid Pant-specific evidence supports them.**

When a conclusion comes from another product, label it `HYPOTHESIS`, name the source product, and define a Fluid Pant test.

## Source-of-truth hierarchy

| Priority | Source | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live Fluid Pant product page | Current price, offer, colors, sizes, availability, shipping, return information, and dynamic customer-facing facts |
| 2 | This Master Context | Evidence semantics, product-specific strategy, and non-negotiable system rules |
| 3 | Supporting files in this directory | Customer, creative, research, prompt, creator, and operational instructions |
| 4 | [Fluid Pant Creative Memory](../fluid_pant.md) | Detailed footage library, hook variants, editing frameworks, historical asset notes, and performance logging |

If sources conflict, follow the higher source and flag the conflict. Never resolve a conflict by guessing.

## Product truth

### **VALIDATED / PRODUCT TRUTH**: Stable functional and visual facts

Use the live product page for stable functional copy and current official imagery for exact visual construction. The Fluid Pant has a relaxed, **wide straight-leg** visual proportion in approved creative references, with pleated/fine-striped texture, natural drape, and a drawstring waist when visible.

The documented visual differentiators are pleats, drape, texture, authentic color/tonal shift under light where applicable, movement, wide straight-leg silhouette, waistband reveal, and pocket interaction.

**Operating implication:** Every creative asset must make at least one visual differentiator readable. The pant is the hero.

### **VALIDATED / OPERATING DECISION**: Dynamic commercial data is never evergreen

Price, offer, current colors, sizes, inventory, shipping, and return terms can change. Check the live product page immediately before using them publicly.

## Current strategy: Wonder → Desire → Proof

### **VALIDATED / OPERATING DECISION**: Product first

Lead with a readable product differentiator rather than generic lifestyle setup, a long talking-head introduction, or delayed product reveal.

### **STRONG SIGNAL**: Wonder through product-relevant novelty

Macro pleats, impossible crop, authentic light/color interaction, low-angle stride, pocket interaction, tactile discovery, and distinctive silhouette are priority hook mechanisms. Predicted rankings are not winners until measured.

Use the benchmark:

> **Which hook immediately communicates that these are not ordinary pants, while making me curious enough to keep watching?**

### **HYPOTHESIS**: Desire connects novelty to personal relevance

Working desire bubbles include appearance, individuality, taste/design literacy, social attention, creative identity, comfort without looking ordinary, versatility/full-day wear, and visual curiosity. These are testable directions, not established customer research. See [Customer Desires](customer/desires.md).

### **VALIDATED / PRODUCT TRUTH**: Proof must be visual

Use movement, texture, silhouette, construction, light interaction, or natural hand/pocket interaction. Generic statements such as `comfortable and versatile` are not sufficient creative proof on their own.

## Customer and creator context

The working creative center is a **design/style-curious buyer who notices visual difference**, not a validated age/income demographic. Potential contexts include creative professionals, musicians, artists, style-forward operators, travel-oriented everyday dressers, and design-curious shoppers. These remain hypotheses until Fluid Pant-specific customer or performance evidence supports them.

Do not infer that older creators, younger fashion creators, entrepreneurs, musicians, or any demographic automatically perform better. Test creator fit using content quality, audience relevance, product visibility, style context, and measured outcomes.

## Creator program boundary

### **VALIDATED / OPERATING DECISION**: Affiliate first, paid UGC separately

Organic affiliate work and paid-media usage are separate. Paid usage, UGC fees, or ad-spend performance bonuses require separate written terms, rights, attribution, and approval.

## Creative non-negotiables

1. The pant is the first thing noticed in a static and appears in the first second of video.
2. Every asset has one central product idea.
3. Environment and creator identity support the product; they do not replace product proof.
4. Preserve the supplied reference: relaxed, wide straight-leg silhouette, real pleated micro-stripe texture, natural drape, and drawstring waist when visible.
5. For AI motion, product fidelity beats dramatic movement. Prefer one natural step, weight shift, subtle turn, or pocket interaction.
6. Do not create liquid/wavy/morphing pleats or animated color gradients. Color shift must arise from believable light on the real fabric.
7. Use real product facts. Avoid unsupported performance, comfort, durability, price, shipping, inventory, or universal-fit claims.
8. Keep work premium but native.
9. Organic creator approval does not grant paid-media rights.

## What must not be invented

- A demographic claim without Fluid Pant-specific evidence.
- A conversion claim, product benefit, or testimonial that is not substantiated.
- Current commercial details without a live check.
- A customer-avatar fact derived from another product.
- Paid creator rights or terms that are not approved.
- Garment construction, hardware, colors, or motion absent from the approved reference.

## Required workflow for new information

1. Identify the source.
2. Assign evidence strength: `VALIDATED/PERFORMANCE-PROVEN`, `STRONG SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `UNTESTED`.
3. Assign decision type where relevant: `PRODUCT TRUTH`, `APPROVED FRAMEWORK`, `OPERATING DECISION`, or `PERFORMANCE RESULT`.
4. Record date, source, decision, and next action in [Testing History](research/testing_history.md) or [Hypotheses](research/hypotheses.md).
5. Update a canonical downstream file only when the conclusion changes team behavior.
6. Never import another product's conclusion without labeling it as a Fluid Pant hypothesis and creating a product-specific test.
