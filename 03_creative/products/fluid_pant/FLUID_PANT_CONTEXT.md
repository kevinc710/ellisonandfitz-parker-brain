# Fluid Pant Master Context

> **Read this first before doing any Fluid Pant work.** Every downstream customer, creative, research, prompt, creator, or operating file must be consistent with this document.

## Purpose

The Ellison Fluid Pant is a product-specific creative and growth system. This file turns scattered product notes, performance analysis, creator discussions, and approved operating decisions into a controlled source of truth.

The objective is not to make every Fluid Pant asset look alike. The objective is to make every asset, creator brief, static, video, and AI prompt **congruent with the product's real visual differentiators and current evidence level**.

## Evidence-status system

Every major claim must carry one of these labels.

| Status | Meaning | How the team may use it |
|---|---|---|
| **VALIDATED** | Supported by live product truth, a documented approved business decision, or repeated Fluid Pant-specific evidence. | Use as a default operating rule. Still cite or link the evidence source. |
| **STRONG SIGNAL** | Supported by meaningful but incomplete Fluid Pant evidence or a clearly recorded strategic pattern. | Use to prioritize testing. Do not state as universal customer truth. |
| **HYPOTHESIS** | A plausible, testable idea that does not yet have enough Fluid Pant-specific evidence. | Use only in a defined test with a success metric. |
| **UNTESTED** | A reasonable question or idea with no Fluid Pant-specific evidence yet. | Do not use as a targeting fact, customer claim, or default creative rule. |

## Absolute rule: no cross-product data contamination

> **Tavo Shorts, Denver Knit, other Ellison & Fitz products, competitor observations, and general apparel ideas may inspire a Fluid Pant hypothesis. They may never be recorded as a Fluid Pant customer or creative truth until Fluid Pant-specific evidence validates them.**

When a conclusion comes from another product, label it `HYPOTHESIS`, name the source product, and define a Fluid Pant test. Never copy a different product's hook, customer insight, claim, or winning format into the Fluid Pant evidence base as if it were proven.

| Priority | Source | Governs |

## Product truth

### **VALIDATED**: The product's stable functional and visual facts

The current live product page describes the Fluid Pant as lightweight and breathable, with striped fabric, a relaxed straight-leg cut, adjustable drawstring waist, machine washable and quick-drying care, and use contexts including everyday wear, travel, and a casual night out.[1]

The visual product differentiators documented in the existing Fluid Pant creative record are pleats, drape, texture, color shift under light, movement, wide-leg silhouette, waistband reveal, and pocket interaction.[2]

**Operating implication:** Every creative asset must make at least one visual differentiator visible. The pant must be the hero, not an afterthought in a lifestyle story.

### **VALIDATED**: Dynamic commercial data is never evergreen

Price, offer, current colors, sizes, inventory, shipping, and return terms can change. Check the live product page before placing any of these details in a creator message, static, video, AI prompt, landing page, or customer communication.

## Current strategy: Wonder → Desire → Proof

### **VALIDATED**: The product-first rule

The existing Fluid Pant creative record designates product-first micro-demonstrations as the approved creative philosophy. Assets should lead with a readable product differentiator rather than a generic lifestyle setup, long talking-head introduction, or delayed product reveal.[2]

### **STRONG SIGNAL**: Wonder is a valuable entry mechanism

Visual novelty and curiosity are the current highest-priority mechanisms for winning the first second. Macro pleats, an unidentifiable crop, color shift, low-angle stride, pocket interaction, and texture pull are documented as priority hook candidates. Some hook rankings remain pre-test predictions and must not be treated as conclusive winners until their Fluid Pant test results are recorded.[2]

### **HYPOTHESIS**: Desire grows when product novelty connects to personal identity

A creator's personal story may increase desire after the product has created initial curiosity, particularly when the story is about individuality, creative life, movement, or wearing something that does not look ordinary. This is a testable creative angle, not an established customer truth.

### **VALIDATED**: Proof must be visual

The strongest proof is product movement, texture, silhouette, or a natural interaction. Generic statements such as "comfortable and versatile" are insufficient on their own because they could describe any pant.

## Customer and creator context

### **STRONG SIGNAL**: Brand-aligned starting audience

The broader brand positioning describes a starting audience of men interested in fitness, entrepreneurship, travel, and self-development. This is a brand-level context, not a complete Fluid Pant-specific customer study.[3]

### **HYPOTHESIS**: Fluid Pant may resonate with individual-expression segments

Potential product-fit contexts include creative professionals, musicians, artists, style-forward entrepreneurs, travel-oriented everyday dressers, and design-curious shoppers. These are useful sources for creator and creative testing because the product's visual differentiation can be demonstrated in their natural worlds. They are not validated Fluid Pant avatars yet.

### **UNTESTED**: Relative creator demographic performance

There is no Fluid Pant-specific evidence yet that older creative-professional creators outperform younger fashion-oriented creators, or that any one demographic segment is universally stronger. Test creator fit using content quality, audience relevance, style context, product visibility, and conversion outcomes rather than demographic assumption.

### **HYPOTHESIS**: Social attention may be a purchase desire

The idea that shoppers want a pant that attracts questions or attention is plausible but unvalidated. Treat this as a messaging test with approved first-person creator language, not a default customer claim.

## Creator program boundary

### **VALIDATED**: Affiliate first, paid UGC separately

The approved operating model starts creators on an organic affiliate pilot with a unique link or customer code. Paid-media usage, UGC fees, or ad-spend performance bonuses require separate written terms, rights, asset attribution, and approval.

**Do not promise:** paid usage, performance bonuses, custom rates, product shipment, or a UGC role during initial creator outreach.

## Creative non-negotiables

1. The pant is the first thing noticed in a static and appears in the first second of video.
2. Every asset has one central product idea: pleats, drape, texture, color shift, silhouette, waistband, or pocket interaction.
3. Environment and creator identity support the product. They do not replace product proof.
4. Use real product facts. Avoid unsupported performance, comfort, durability, price, shipping, inventory, or universal-fit claims.
5. Keep work premium but native. A slightly raw, product-clear asset can outperform an elaborate fashion film.
6. Organic creator approval does not grant paid-media rights.

## What must not be invented

- A demographic claim without Fluid Pant-specific evidence.
- A conversion claim, product benefit, or testimonial that is not approved or substantiated.
- Current price, discount, shipping time, availability, or size information without a live check.
- A customer avatar fact derived only from another product's data or a single ChatGPT conversation.
- A paid creator term that has not been approved in writing.

## Required workflow for new information

1. Identify the source: live product page, Fluid Pant performance data, creator result, customer review, survey, competitor research, internal decision, or raw idea.
2. Assign a status: `VALIDATED`, `STRONG SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `UNTESTED`.
3. Record the date, source, decision, and next action in [Research: Testing History](research/testing_history.md) or [Research: Hypotheses](research/hypotheses.md).
4. Update a canonical downstream file only when the new conclusion changes how the team should act.
5. Never copy a conclusion from another product without labeling it as a Fluid Pant hypothesis and creating a test.
