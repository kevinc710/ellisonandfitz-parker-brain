# Fluid Pant Creative Operating System

## The operating principle

> **The pant is the hero.** The creator, setting, and copy exist to make the pant's pleats, drape, texture, color shift, movement, and silhouette easier to notice.

The goal is not to make a generic fashion ad. The goal is to create one clear visual or verbal reason to look twice at the Fluid Pant.

## Non-negotiable creative rules

1. Show the product or its unique differentiator in the first second of a video and the first glance of a static.
2. Build around **one idea per asset**. Do not combine comfort, travel, fabric, personal identity, fit, and price in a single creative.
3. Use a real visual proof: pleat texture, drape during movement, wide-leg silhouette, color shift, waistband, or pocket interaction.
4. Keep environments secondary. A good setting gives context but does not overpower the pant.
5. Keep the work premium but native. A slightly raw, conversational asset can be better than an overly polished fashion film.
6. Verify all dynamic details on the live product page before use.
7. Use the detailed [Fluid Pant Creative Memory](../fluid_pant.md) for tested footage, hooks, frameworks, named clips, and paid-creative results.

## Static-ad system

### What a high-potential Fluid Pant static does

A strong static makes the silhouette, texture, or color shift readable before a viewer reads a headline. Text, if used, is short and supports what the image already proves.

| Static format | Visual setup | Optional headline direction | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silhouette first | Full-body or waist-to-hem image, clear wide-leg shape, clean environment | `THE PANTS THAT MOVE DIFFERENT` | Headshot-heavy composition or busy backgrounds |
| Texture first | Macro pleats or hand interaction with light catching the fabric | `LOOK CLOSER` | A flat, poorly lit fabric close-up |
| Movement first | Mid-stride, turn, stair movement, or wind catching the fabric | `MADE TO BE NOTICED IN MOTION` | Static pose with no visible product story |
| Color-shift first | Detail under directional light where tonal change is obvious | `NOT YOUR USUAL PANTS` | Copy that describes colors the image cannot show |
| Styling first | One pant shown in two or three distinct contexts with the same product visible | `ONE PIECE. YOUR WAY.` | A collage that makes the pant too small to notice |

### Static design rules

- Crop to keep the pant visually dominant. Waist to hem, close texture, or a readable full silhouette are preferred.
- Use simple background contrast so the silhouette and colorway remain legible.
- Keep text to one main claim plus a small supporting line or CTA when needed.
- Do not make an asset look like a department-store catalog card unless it is a deliberate offer or comparison test.
- Do not use generic beige minimalism if it erases the color, pleats, or product personality.
- Do not use text that makes an unsupported comfort, performance, or product-comparison claim.

## Video and creator-content system

### Product-first structure

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 1.0 seconds | Movement, texture, pocket, waistband, color shift, or a full silhouette appears immediately |
| 1.0 to 3.0 seconds | Optional concise creator statement or a second proof point |
| 3.0 seconds onward | Return to walking, turn, close-up, styling context, or a complete look |
| Closing moment | Clear full silhouette, visual end frame, or a concise call to action if briefed |

### Creator-led concepts

| Angle | Creator starting line | Product proof immediately after |
|---|---|---|
| Individuality | `I have never been interested in dressing like everybody else.` | Movement and full silhouette |
| Creative life | `I want what I wear to have something to say too.` | Rehearsal, studio, city, or evening styling sequence |
| Genuine discovery | `These are some of the most interesting pants in my closet right now.` | Texture, detail, movement, and full look |
| Styling versatility | `Here is how I would wear one piece three different ways.` | Three readable looks, each with pant visible |

Do not force these lines on a creator. They are structure examples. The creator should speak in a natural voice and only make claims they genuinely stand behind.

## AI prompt framework

Use this structure instead of a vague scene description:

```text
Create a [FORMAT] for the Ellison & Fitz Fluid Pant.

PRODUCT PRIORITY: The pants must be the first thing noticed and occupy [PERCENTAGE] of the frame when practical.

ONE VISUAL IDEA: [PLEATS / DRAPE / COLOR SHIFT / WIDE-LEG SILHOUETTE / POCKET INTERACTION].

CAMERA: [LOW ANGLE / WAIST HEIGHT / MACRO / CHEST-DOWN POV / HANDHELD DISCOVERY].

ACTION: [MID-STRIDE / TURN / HAND BRUSHES FABRIC / WALKING / MIRROR REVEAL].

SETTING: [MINIMAL ARCHITECTURE / CREATIVE STUDIO / TRAVEL TRANSIT / CITY STREET / EVENING CONTEXT]. Keep it secondary to the product.

STYLE: Premium but native, clean directional light, no embedded text, no logos, no unsupported product claims.

FORMAT: [9:16 VIDEO / 4:5 STATIC / 1:1 STATIC].
```

### Example static prompt

```text
Create a 4:5 editorial static for the Ellison & Fitz Fluid Pant. The visual idea is wide-leg silhouette and fabric movement. Show a style-conscious creative person mid-stride in a clean concrete setting. Frame from waist to hem so the pant occupies most of the image. The pleated fabric should catch directional light and show a visible color shift. Premium but native fashion-documentary feel. Minimal background. No text, no logos, no accessories that compete with the pant.
```

### Example video prompt

```text
Create a 9:16 handheld product-discovery video for the Ellison & Fitz Fluid Pant. Start in the first frame with a low-angle mid-stride shot where the wide-leg silhouette and pleated fabric are clearly visible. The pant should occupy at least 60% of the frame. Then cut to a close-up of a hand entering the pocket and briefly catching the pleated texture in directional light. End on a full silhouette walking away. Premium but native feel, movement already in progress, no static setup, no embedded text, no voiceover.
```

## What not to make

- A generic talking head where the pant appears late or barely appears at all.
- A creator monologue longer than the product demonstration.
- A static with large copy and a tiny, unreadable product.
- A generic athleisure ad that could sell any pant.
- A polished fashion film with slow, empty setup before the product appears.
- A concept whose only proof is "comfortable and versatile."
- A creator asset that implies paid use, performance bonus, or product claims without approved terms.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Is the pant the first thing noticed?
- [ ] Does the asset demonstrate one unique Fluid Pant attribute?
- [ ] Are all claims allowed and supported?
- [ ] Is the setting secondary to the product?
- [ ] Is current pricing, offer, availability, or shipping information live-checked if included?
- [ ] Does a creator asset contain correct link, code, tag, and disclosure if required?
- [ ] Are paid-media rights confirmed before any organic creator asset is used in an ad?
