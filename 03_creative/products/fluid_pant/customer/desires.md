# Fluid Pant Customer Desires

> Do not present any statement in this file as customer research unless its status is `VALIDATED`. Use `HYPOTHESIS` and `UNTESTED` statements only to design explicit tests.

## Desire map

| Desire | Status | Why it may matter | Safe creative expression | Test needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See an immediately distinctive pant | **STRONG SIGNAL** | The Fluid Pant's current creative record prioritizes visual novelty, texture, color shift, drape, and silhouette as scroll-stop mechanisms. | Product-first macro, movement, or silhouette hooks. | Compare against generic lifestyle openings. |
| Feel comfortable without looking ordinary | **HYPOTHESIS** | The live product page supports ease-related product facts; the "not ordinary" identity framing is not yet customer-validated. | `Comfort without blending in` as a test theme, supported by real product visuals. | Measure conversion and comment quality against product-only control. |
| Express personal taste | **HYPOTHESIS** | The product is visually distinctive and creator strategy includes individuality angles. | First-person creator observation about personal style. | Test with creators who have a clear, real style point of view. |
| Have a pant that moves through a full day | **HYPOTHESIS** | The product page lists everyday wear, travel, and casual night-out contexts. | Day-in-the-life or movement sequences with product proof. | Compare travel or movement context with texture control. |
| Receive social attention for a visually unusual item | **UNTESTED** | No direct Fluid Pant customer evidence has yet been recorded. | Do not state as a customer fact. May test first-person phrasing only. | Test `people ask about these` against a product-only control. |
| Signal creativity or individuality | **HYPOTHESIS** | Creative-persona content may make product differentiation feel credible. | Music, studio, city, or personal-style context that keeps the pant visible. | Compare conversion and creator quality across contexts. |

## Message rules

### Use now

Use visible product proof and factual, first-person language:

- `The pleats move differently when you walk.`
- `I like how the fabric catches the light.`
- `These are some of the most interesting pants in my closet right now.`
- `I do not want to dress like everybody else.`

The speaker must genuinely stand behind a first-person statement, and the visual must demonstrate the product.

### Do not use as a default

- `Everyone asks me where I got these.`
- `These make you look more creative.`
- `The most comfortable pants you will own.`
- `Perfect for any occasion.`

These claims are not validated or are too broad. They may only be used after evidence and approval, if ever.

## Voice-of-customer collection plan

The team needs product-specific language before creating a `voice_of_customer.md` file. Collect exactly quoted, attributable language from:

1. Fluid Pant reviews.
2. Post-purchase survey answers.
3. Customer support and return notes, with sensitive data removed.
4. Creator comments and DMs, with source and date recorded.
5. Landing-page or ad comments that refer to the product itself.

For each entry, record the quote, date, source type, theme, and whether it indicates desire, objection, surprise, or confusion. Do not invent customer quotes from AI analysis.
