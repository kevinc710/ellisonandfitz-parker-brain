# Fluid Pant Customer Desires

> Do not present any statement in this file as customer research unless its status is `VALIDATED`. Use `STRONG SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, and `UNTESTED` statements to guide tests without turning inference into customer truth.

## Working center of market

The current product-specific creative evidence points most strongly toward a **design/style-curious buyer who notices visual difference**. This is a working creative center, not a validated demographic profile. The product earns attention through pleats, drape, silhouette, movement, texture, and applicable color shift before any identity message is added.

## Message rules

### Use now

Use visible product proof and factual or genuinely subjective first-person language:

- `The pleats move differently when you walk.`
- `I like how the fabric catches the light.`
- `These are some of the most interesting pants in my closet right now.`
- `I do not want to dress like everybody else.`

The speaker must genuinely stand behind a first-person statement, and the visual must demonstrate the product.

### Test carefully; do not treat as customer truth

- `These don't look like normal pants.`
- `I keep getting asked about these.`
- `This is what I wear when I want a simple outfit to look more intentional.`
- `These have more personality than most pants in my closet.`

These can be tested as authentic creator lines or ad copy where appropriate, but they do not become customer research merely because they perform.

### Do not use as a default

- `Everyone asks me where I got these.`
- `These make you look more creative.`
- `The most comfortable pants you will own.`
- `Perfect for any occasion.`

These claims are unsupported, universal, or too broad unless separately evidenced and approved.

## Voice-of-customer collection plan

The team needs product-specific language before promoting desire hypotheses to customer truth. Collect exactly quoted, attributable language from:

1. Fluid Pant reviews.
2. Post-purchase survey answers.
3. Customer support and return notes, with sensitive data removed.
4. Creator comments and DMs, with source and date recorded.
5. Landing-page or ad comments that refer to the Fluid Pant itself.

For each entry, record the quote, date, source type, theme, and whether it indicates desire, objection, surprise, confusion, social attention, appearance, comfort, versatility, or identity. Do not invent customer quotes from AI analysis.
