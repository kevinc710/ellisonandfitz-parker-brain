# Fluid Pant Core ICP

> **Governing source:** [Fluid Pant Master Context](../FLUID_PANT_CONTEXT.md). This file records only Fluid Pant-specific evidence, approved product context, and clearly labeled hypotheses.

## Current conclusion

There is not yet a fully validated, Fluid Pant-specific customer profile. The team should not treat a broad brand target as confirmed product-level evidence.

## Evidence table

| Claim | Status | Evidence | Operating use |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Fluid Pant's strongest visual differentiators are pleats, drape, texture, color shift, movement, and silhouette. | **VALIDATED** | Product page and Fluid Pant Creative Memory | Build creative around a visible differentiator. |

| The Fluid Pant is relevant to people who value individual expression and do not want ordinary-looking clothing. | **HYPOTHESIS** | Product differentiation and current creator strategy | Test identity-led messaging against product-first controls. |
| One demographic group is the highest-value Fluid Pant buyer. | **UNTESTED** | No Fluid Pant-specific cohort or conversion analysis yet | Do not target or message as if this is known. |

## Product-level ICP working statement

> **HYPOTHESIS:** The most promising Fluid Pant customer is a style-conscious person who notices visual product novelty, wants ease without blending into generic clothing, and responds to proof through movement, texture, silhouette, and personal styling context.

This is a working test definition. It must not be turned into a fixed demographic fact until it is supported by Fluid Pant customer, conversion, or ad-performance evidence.

## What the core ICP is likely to value

| Potential desire | Status | How to test it |
|---|---|---|
| A piece that looks visually distinct from ordinary pants | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Compare novelty-led product hooks against generic lifestyle hooks. |
| Comfortable, easy styling that still feels intentional | **HYPOTHESIS** | Test message combinations that pair ease with visual distinction. |
| A product that works across creative work, travel, daily movement, and evening plans | **HYPOTHESIS** | Test context-specific creative and customer response. |
| Social attention or being asked about the pants | **HYPOTHESIS** | Test first-person social-attention lines against a product-only control. |

## What the team must not assume

- That a creator's age, profession, follower count, or style label proves their audience will convert.
- That all Fluid Pant customers want the same identity story.
- That interest in fashion means interest in the Fluid Pant specifically.
- That a broad brand audience is a validated Fluid Pant purchaser cohort.

## Evidence needed to validate the ICP

The team should prioritize Fluid Pant-specific evidence from:

1. Shopify order and cohort data, segmented carefully by available information.
2. Meta creative performance tagged specifically to Fluid Pant.
3. Post-purchase survey or review language tied to the Fluid Pant.
4. Creator-level clicks, conversion, and content-quality results.
5. Customer-service or return themes that mention fit, fabric, styling, color, or expectations.

When evidence is collected, update this file with date, source, sample size or scope, and the decision it changes.
