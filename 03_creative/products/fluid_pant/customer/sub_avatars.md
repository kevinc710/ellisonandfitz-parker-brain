# Fluid Pant Sub-Avatars

> Every sub-avatar below is a **testable context**, not a validated demographic target. Do not use it as a targeting rule until Fluid Pant-specific evidence supports it.

## Active sub-avatar hypotheses

| Sub-avatar context | Status | Product tension | Strongest visual proof | Appropriate creator context | Test question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creative individualist | **HYPOTHESIS** | Wants personal style without looking costumed or generic | Product-first silhouette, texture, and a concise style statement | Artist, musician, designer, stylist, photographer | Does an individuality-led creator context improve qualified attention or conversion versus product-only creative? |
| Movement-led everyday dresser | **HYPOTHESIS** | Wants ease through travel, errands, commuting, or a long day without losing visual interest | Walking, turn, stair, pocket, and drape shots | Travel, lifestyle, city, active daily-life creator | Does product movement outperform a static styling context? |
| Style-forward entrepreneur | **HYPOTHESIS** | Wants to look deliberate without feeling rigid or uniform | Architecture, clean silhouette, work-to-evening styling | Founder, freelancer, creative operator, consultant | Does intentional-style framing generate better response than generic professional styling? |
| Design-curious explorer | **STRONG SIGNAL** | Notices texture, proportion, material, and visual novelty | Macro pleats, color shift, visual puzzle, fabric interaction | Fashion, art, product-design, visual-culture creator | Which novelty hook generates strongest thumb-stop and 3-second hold? |
| Established creative professional | **UNTESTED** | May value distinct personal style and product quality over trend conformity | Product-first performance, studio, travel, or evening context | Musician, performer, creative leader | Does this creator context outperform other contexts for Fluid Pant affiliate or UGC results? |

## Rules for using sub-avatars

1. Select a sub-avatar based on the creator's visible world and a defined test objective, not personal inference.
2. Test one major variable at a time. Do not change creator type, hook, price, offer, and message simultaneously.
3. Give each test a product-first control when possible.
4. Record results in [Research: Testing History](../research/testing_history.md).
5. Promote a sub-avatar from `HYPOTHESIS` to `STRONG SIGNAL` only when at least one documented Fluid Pant test produces a meaningful, repeatable result.
6. Promote a sub-avatar to `VALIDATED` only after repeated evidence across relevant metrics or a sufficient customer-data study.

## Prohibited shortcut

Do not rename a broad demographic as an avatar and treat it as a strategy. "Older men," "younger fashion buyers," or any other demographic category does not explain the product tension, visual proof, or reason the Fluid Pant should matter.

The correct unit of analysis is: **context + tension + product proof + outcome**.
