## The source-of-truth rule

The **live product page** controls price, product availability, sizes, colorways, shipping, returns, and other commercial details that may change. The documents in this directory provide the evergreen strategy, creative rules, and approved product language.

**Preferred live product page:** https://ellisonandfitz.com/products/ellison-fluid-pant

Never hard-code dynamic commercial details into a creator message, static, video, or AI prompt unless they were confirmed on the live page immediately before use.

## How to use this with AI

Do not paste a raw ChatGPT transcript into a team prompt and assume it is current. Instead, copy the relevant portion of the **AI Context Pack** and add the assignment. Include the product page when current price, availability, or colorway information matters.

Example:

```text
Use the Fluid Pant AI Context Pack below. Create three 9:16 static-ad concepts for a style-conscious creative professional. Keep the pant as the first thing noticed. Do not use generic comfort claims, current price, delivery promises, or age-based assumptions. Each concept must use one product differentiator only.

[PASTE AI CONTEXT PACK]
```

## How to maintain this system

1. Share new learning, ChatGPT output, customer research, creator feedback, or ad results with the owner or agent.
2. Curate the useful conclusion into the correct file. Do not paste raw conversations directly into canonical strategy files.
3. Date every changing performance or audience fact.
4. Update the Learning Log after a meaningful test, creator pilot, or creative batch.
5. Keep the existing [Fluid Pant Creative Memory](../fluid_pant.md) as the detailed performance and asset record rather than copying its footage library here.

## Non-negotiable rules

- The pant is the hero. The person, setting, and copy support the product.
- Start with a product differentiator, not a generic lifestyle setup.
- Keep one idea per asset.
- Do not promise paid usage or UGC compensation in affiliate outreach.
- Do not segment or message people based on assumed age, identity, income, or lifestyle. Use their visible creative context, audience fit, and content ability.
- Do not use generic claims such as "the most comfortable pant" unless approved and substantiated.
