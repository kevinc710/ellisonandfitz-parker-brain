# Fluid Pant Creator Prompt Library

## Creator brief foundation

Every creator assignment must state whether it is:

1. **Organic affiliate content:** Creator uses a unique link or code. No paid-media usage is granted.
2. **Paid UGC:** Creator produces content for brand use under separately approved compensation and rights terms.
3. **Paid-media test:** A specific approved asset is used with written usage rights, asset ID, and performance tracking.

Do not combine these categories or imply that organic affiliate content automatically becomes paid media.

## Organic affiliate brief template

```text
PRODUCT: Ellison Fluid Pant

OBJECTIVE: Create one natural, product-first vertical asset that makes a real Fluid Pant differentiator visible in your own environment.

YOUR ANGLE: [CHOOSE ONE: movement / pleated texture / color shift / silhouette / personal style / travel context / creative-life context].

PRODUCT-FIRST RULE: Show the pant in the opening second. Your setting and personal perspective should support the product, not delay it.

REQUIRED VISUAL PROOF: [CHOOSE ONE OR TWO: walking or turn; full silhouette; close texture; pocket interaction; waistband detail].

YOUR VOICE: Speak naturally and only share your genuine opinion. Avoid generic claims such as "the most comfortable pants ever" or claims about price, shipping, inventory, or universal fit.

POSTING: Use your assigned link and customer code. Tag [CURRENT BRAND HANDLE]. Add the required affiliate disclosure.

RIGHTS: This assignment is for your organic affiliate content only. Do not assume paid-media usage or UGC performance terms unless separate written terms are provided.
```

## Identity-led creator prompt

Use only after the pant is visible.

```text
Open with the Fluid Pant in motion or a strong product detail. Then share one short, natural thought about personal style, creative life, travel, or not wanting to dress like everybody else. Return immediately to movement, texture, or a full silhouette. Do not read a script word for word. Do not make a product claim you cannot personally support.
```

## Movement-led creator prompt

```text
Open with a walk, turn, step, or travel moment that makes the Fluid Pant's drape visible. Include one full-look shot and one close-up of texture, pocket, or waistband. Keep the setting real and uncluttered. The video should feel like a genuine discovery, not a staged commercial.
```

## Texture-led creator prompt

```text
Open with the pleated fabric close enough to make the viewer curious. Use natural light and a light hand interaction to show texture. Then reveal the full pant and how it moves when you walk. Keep the product centered and do not cover it with props or extra styling.
```

## Creator QA before delivery

- [ ] The Fluid Pant appears in the opening second.
- [ ] At least one visual differentiator is clear.
- [ ] The product has not been visually altered, misdescribed, or hidden.
- [ ] The message is natural and first-person when opinion is used.
- [ ] The assigned link, code, tag, and disclosure are included if posting organically.
- [ ] No paid-media or UGC compensation promise is implied.
- [ ] Any product claim, price, sale, or shipping statement has been approved and live-checked.
