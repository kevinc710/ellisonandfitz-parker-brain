# Standing Creative Brief — Ellison & Fitz

Use this as the default brief for any new ad creative request unless the
person gives different direction.

**Audience:** Men, 22–35, into fitness, entrepreneurship, travel,
self-development.

**Positioning:** Comfort + function + elevated minimalism.

**Format defaults:**
- Sub-10 second cuts
- POV / handheld
- No voiceover
- Lead with fabric texture / product-in-motion, not lifestyle-first shots

**Hero product:** Ellison Fluid Pant (see `01_brand_foundation/product_lines.md`
for colorway performance data — Mixed Color, Violet Blue, Royal Blue, and
Fluorescent Green are the proven top sellers, useful as safe defaults when a
colorway isn't specified).

**Reference boards:** Foreplay — `#ugc_formats`, `#hooks`, `#offers`,
`#product_showcase`.

**Editor:** Daniela executes final cuts.
