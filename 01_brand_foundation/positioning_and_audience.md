# Positioning & Audience

## What Ellison & Fitz is

Ellison & Fitz (legal entity: DTC Commerce Group LLC) is a Shopify-based
DTC apparel brand run as a hybrid model: a mix of dropshipping and
branded/owned apparel lines. Co-founded and co-run by Kevin and Leandro
Marques.

## Positioning

Ellison & Fitz sits at the intersection of **comfort, function, and elevated
minimalism**. The brand's signature product family is the **Ellison Fluid
Pant** — a comfort-forward, function-first pant line that anchors the
apparel catalog.

## Target audience

Men, roughly ages 22–35, interested in:
- Fitness
- Entrepreneurship
- Travel
- Self-development

## Brand voice / creative philosophy

Product-led, motion-first video advertising:
- Sub-10 second cuts
- POV / handheld camera angles
- No voiceover
- Fabric texture as the primary visual hook
