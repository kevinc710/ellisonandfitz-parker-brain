# Ellison & Fitz — Brand Identity Guidelines
_Extracted from Brand Guidelines PDF (2026 Edition)_

---

## 01. TYPOGRAPHY

### Primary Font — Montserrat
- Used for: Headlines, titles, navigation, display text, ad overlay headlines
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)

### Secondary Font — Nunito Sans
- Used for: Body copy, product descriptions, UI elements, supporting ad overlay text, callouts
- Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)

### Tertiary Fonts
None. The brand operates on a two-font system only.

### Ad Creative Usage
Montserrat and Nunito Sans are the confirmed fonts for text overlays on both video and static ad creatives. No separate ad-specific typeface exists.
- Montserrat carries the headline/hook weight
- Nunito Sans handles supporting copy and callouts

### Primary Colors
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Jet Black | #0A0A0A | Primary backgrounds, logo on dark |
| Deep Black | #1A1A1A | Dark background variant |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Light backgrounds, logo on light |
| Warm Gold | #D4A574 | Accent, highlights, premium detail |

### Secondary / Neutral Colors
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud Gray | #F5F5F5 | Light background variant |
| Slate | #666666 | Secondary text, supporting copy |
| Ash | #999999 | Tertiary text, captions |
| Charcoal | #2A2A2A | Mid-dark backgrounds |
| Bronze | #9D7559 | Warm accent, secondary gold |

### Primary Brand Color
Jet Black (#0A0A0A) — the dominant color across all brand touchpoints.

### Ad Background Colors
- Dark ads: #0A0A0A or #1A1A1A
- Light ads: #FFFFFF or #F5F5F5
- The brand skews dark-background-forward given the quiet luxury positioning.

### Off-Brand Colors — Never Use
- Neon colors of any kind
- Loud primaries (red, bright orange, electric blue) used as dominant colors
- Pastels
- Any color associated with hype, streetwear, or fast fashion aesthetics
- Colors that read as "discount" or "sale" (e.g. aggressive red sale banners)

## 04. AD CREATIVE VISUAL RULES

### Text Overlay Style
- Headline font: Montserrat Bold (700) or SemiBold (600)
- Supporting copy: Nunito Sans Regular (400) or SemiBold (600)
- Text color on dark backgrounds: Pure White (#FFFFFF) or Warm Gold (#D4A574) for accent
- Text color on light backgrounds: Jet Black (#0A0A0A) or Charcoal (#2A2A2A)
- Keep overlays minimal — the product is always the visual hero, not the copy
- High contrast between text and background is required at all times

### Motion & Animation Style (Video)
- Sub-10 second cuts — tight, fast, no padding
- POV / handheld camera angles — intentional, not shaky
- No voiceover — product does the talking visually
- Lead with fabric texture and product-in-motion within the first 1–3 seconds
- Movement is the primary visual hook: pleats, drape, fabric behavior in motion
- No heavy cinematic storytelling or lifestyle-first openers
- Faceless formats and product-showcase formats are the current core

### Aspect Ratios
| Ratio | Platform Use |
|---|---|
| 9:16 | Instagram Reels, TikTok, Stories — primary video format |
| 1:1 | Feed static ads — primary static format |
| 4:5 | Feed video and static — secondary format |
| 16:9 | Not a primary format for this brand |

### Visual Elements — Always Used
- Product as the immediate hero (visible within first 1–3 seconds in video; dominant in frame for statics)
- High visual contrast between product and background
- Minimal, clean environments (architectural, neutral, natural light)
- Fabric texture and movement as the core visual story
- Clean negative space — no clutter competing with the garment

### Visual Elements — Never Used
- Loud logos or heavy branding overlaid on creative
- Neon or hype-adjacent color treatments
- Gym mirrors, protein shakers, or fitness-culture aesthetics
- Excessive text overlays that compete with the product
- Generic lifestyle B-roll where the clothing is secondary
- Talking-head openers before the product is shown
- Flashy watches, nightclub settings, or flex-culture imagery
- Discount banners, sale stickers, or promotional overlays in brand creative
